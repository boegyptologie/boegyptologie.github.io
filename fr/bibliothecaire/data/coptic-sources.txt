BOE modifications (2026-11-01): normalized lookup keys to lowercase NFD without
combining marks; combined the Sahidic and Bohairic tables; excluded every key
listed as ambiguous, conflicting normalized analyses, non-Coptic entries and
non-concatenative analyses; retained only multi-unit groups; replaced each
segmentation with code-point boundary offsets to reduce transfer size.

This is a conservative lookup reference, not the full Coptic NLP pipeline.
It does not infer a part of speech, lemma, translation, or contextual reading.
Unknown groups and excluded ambiguities remain unsplit. Absence from an
ambiguity list is not a claim that a form has only one possible analysis.
The original BOE passages remain unchanged.
