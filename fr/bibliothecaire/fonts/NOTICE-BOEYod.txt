BOE Yod Regular

This small webfont is a renamed subset of Charis Regular 7.000,
Copyright (c) 1997-2025 SIL Global (https://www.sil.org/).
Original font: https://software.sil.org/charis/download/
Source release: https://github.com/silnrsi/font-charis/releases/tag/v7.000
License: SIL Open Font License 1.1 (see OFL-BOEYod.txt).

Subset prepared for BOE in November 2026 using fonttools 4.60.1.
Only U+A7BC and U+A7BD (Egyptological yod) are retained.
The glyph outlines are unchanged. The family and PostScript names were
changed to BOE Yod / BOEYod-Regular to respect Reserved Font Names.
The original copyright and license metadata are retained.
Other characters continue using the existing fonts.
